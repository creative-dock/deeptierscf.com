What you get

Sub-tier liquidity you don't have to fund

  • Suppliers funded without your cash. No early payment, no discounting, no facility of your own.
  • Nothing changes on your side. Same terms, same due dates, nothing on your balance sheet.
  • A stronger position with the anchor as the tier 1 whose sub-tier stays reliable.
All we need from you

Read-only access to the orders you already handle, so we can validate both sides of the chain.

  • The anchor's orders to you. Proof the demand is real and well rated.
  • Your orders to tier 2. Proof the invoice they raise answers a real order.

That is the whole ask. No facility, no guarantee, no programme to operate.
