01 • The problem

Liquidity stops at tier 1. The risk doesn't.

Tier 2+ companies cannot get the terms their anchors get. Most are unrated, and none of them hold a direct commercial relationship with the anchor, so funding programmes never cover them. What is left is a commercial loan at a higher rate, or a credit limit smaller than the purchase order already in hand.

No direct line to the anchor

  • The anchor buys from tier 1, not from you.
  • Your work is priced inside someone else's invoice.
  • Funding programmes stop at that line.

Priced on your size, not the demand

  • Unrated, so the bank prices against your own balance sheet.
  • Rates track how small you are.
  • Credit limits land under the order you just won.

The anchor cannot see any of it

  • Visibility ends at the suppliers it invoices directly.
  • Deep-tier stress surfaces as a missed delivery.
  • By then it is a stopped line, priced in millions a day.

The gap

The credit quality sits at the top of the chain. The capital need sits at the bottom, and nothing connects the two.

The issues

03 • The solution

We build the verified transaction record that links your invoice to the anchor's demand. The funder lends against that record.

No programme, just evidence: with permissioned access to documents already moving between ERP systems, we rebuild the chain behind a single invoice.

Verification, not self-declaration

We sit between the parties as an independent layer, corroborating that the invoice, the purchase orders and the delivery all describe one real transaction. When they don't, we say so.
