What neither option recognises

The money behind your invoice comes from an investment grade company two steps up the chain. Until now there was no way to prove it.

  • Your rating sets your rate, not the chain's.
  • Collateral demanded for demand-backed work.
  • Limits that cap growth instead of tracking your order book.
  • Weeks of underwriting when the material is needed now.
What you get

Working capital that arrives when the order does

Sometimes the purchase order is far bigger than anything you forecast. That is when the chain breaks, and that is what this is for.

Cash when your invoice is accepted

Funded once your tier 1 customer accepts the invoice, rather than 60 to 120 days later. Plan production on money you can actually see.

Priced on the anchor's credit rating

We connect your transaction to the credit standing of the OEM your parts are built for, so the rate reflects theirs and not your size.

No programme to join

Apply invoice by invoice. No facility agreement, no minimum volume, no personal guarantees, and nothing to negotiate with the anchor.
